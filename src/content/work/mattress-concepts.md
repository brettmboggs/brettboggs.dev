Mattress Concepts is a family mattress store on High Ridge Blvd, with a workshop behind the showroom. Clinton Smith builds mattresses there for RVs, boats, antique beds and horse trailers. Customers love him: 4.8 stars on Google. The website and the Google listing weren't bringing many of them in.

Site: [mattressconcepts.com](https://www.mattressconcepts.com). Work done late September to October 2026.

## What was wrong

**The site was slow.** An old Wix site that took 8.6 seconds to show its main content on a phone. Some pages still had Wix placeholder text next to "Add to Cart" buttons. Google had been told to hide the Adjustable Beds page, the most expensive products in the store.

**People only found it if they knew the name.** In a month the old site got 108 visits, mostly people typing "mattress concepts." For "custom mattress" it sat on page two or three.

**A custom order meant a phone call.** The contact form had nowhere for measurements or photos.

**The Google profile was out of date.** Wrong hours, a years-old description, no products or services, and the last photo added about four and a half years earlier.

## What was done

1. **A new site, built around how people shop there.** Thirty-two pages: one for each mattress on the floor, plus RV, boat, antique-bed, horse-trailer and two-sided mattresses. Every photo is the real store and Clinton's hands at work. Phone first.
2. **A custom quote form.** As the customer types measurements, it draws the mattress to scale, including cut corners, rounded corners and boat V-berths. Photos attach, and the request lands in the store's inbox.
3. **A switchover that kept the ranking.** Same address. Every old page points to its new one. Registered with Google and Bing the day it launched.
4. **The Google profile, brought up to date.** Hours, holiday closures, a new description, services, five products with photos, six store photos, social links, and the first new posts.
5. **Listed on Bing Maps**, imported from the Google profile.
6. **Tools for reviews.** A counter card with a QR code that opens the review form, a ready-to-send text asking for a review, and sample replies.
7. **The owners control everything.** The domain stayed in their name. Nothing is locked to one person.
