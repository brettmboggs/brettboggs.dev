Wix makes it easy to start a site. It doesn't make it fast, and it charges every month for as long as you keep it. The calculator on [my homepage](/#overhead) uses $39 a month for a website builder, a typical list price in October 2026.

## What Mattress Concepts got

[Mattress Concepts](/work/websites/mattress-concepts/), a family mattress store and workshop in High Ridge, left an old Wix site with placeholder text still on the pages.

| | Old Wix site | New site |
|---|---|---|
| Phone speed score | 74 | 95 |
| Main content on a phone | 8.6 s | 2.7 s |
| Accessibility score | 87 | 100 |
| Monthly hosting | Wix plan | Nothing |

Plus 32 pages built around how people shop there, and a quote form that draws the custom mattress to scale as the customer types.

## How the move goes

I rebuild the site from your real photos and reviews, map every old Wix address to its new page, switch the domain over, and register the new site with Google and Bing the same day. Then you cancel Wix. [More on websites](/help/websites/).
