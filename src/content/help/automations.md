The best place to save time is the work you do the same way every day. Not the hard decisions. The copying, chasing and reminding.

## Common ones

- **Missed-call text-back.** A call you can't take gets a text within a minute, so the customer doesn't call the next name on the list.
- **Quote follow-ups.** A quote that sits for three days gets a polite nudge. Then another. Then it tells you it went cold.
- **Appointment reminders.** Fewer no-shows, without anyone dialing.
- **Online orders into the books.** No more typing the same sale twice.
- **Review requests.** A finished job gets a text asking for a review, with the link.
- **Paperwork read for you.** Receipts, invoices and voicemails turned into lines in your books or work orders. This is where AI earns its keep.
- **The Friday report.** The numbers you build by hand every week, built for you.

## How it goes

I watch the process as it runs today, write down every step, and agree with you what should stay human. Then I build it, run it alongside the old way for a week or two, and switch over when you've seen it work.

One process at a time. The first one should pay for itself before we start the second.
