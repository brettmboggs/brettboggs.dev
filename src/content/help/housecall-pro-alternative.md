Housecall Pro is a good product built for every home-service business at once. That's the catch. You pay for the features everyone else needs, the price climbs as you add people, and the way it works becomes the way you work.

The calculator on [my homepage](/#overhead) uses $189 a month for a scheduling app like this, a typical list price in October 2026. Check what you actually pay, then multiply by five years.

## What replaces it

One system that does the parts you actually use:

- **Booking** from your website, into your calendar
- **Quotes and deposits** that follow themselves up
- **Invoices and payments**
- **Customers** and their full history in one place
- **Reminders and review requests** that send themselves
- **An app on your phone** for the driveway or the job site

You own the code, the data and every login. Adding a tech or a truck costs nothing.

## It's been done

[Knock Em' Down Detailing](/work/websites/ked-detailing/), a mobile detailer in St. Louis, runs on one system built to replace Housecall Pro and QuickBooks: booking, quotes, invoicing, the books, follow-ups, and an iPhone app.

## How the switch goes

1. **A free look.** Walk me through one job, first call to getting paid.
2. **A written plan** with a fixed price.
3. **The build**, while Housecall Pro keeps running.
4. **Cancel**, once you've seen the new one handle real work.
