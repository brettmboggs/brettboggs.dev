Squarespace templates look good in the demo and generic once they're yours. The monthly fee never ends. The calculator on [my homepage](/#overhead) uses $39 a month for a website builder, a typical list price in October 2026.

## What Knock Em' Down got

[Knock Em' Down Detailing](/work/websites/ked-detailing/), a mobile detailer in St. Louis, had a Squarespace template that never got finished. The page title still said "Your Site Title." The reviews on it were made up; the real ones weren't there.

The new site leads with his own 4K footage and his actual reviews, with names. Every old address still resolves. He posts to it himself. Quotes and booking happen on the site now, and it's hosted for nothing.

## How the move goes

Your photos and reviews in, every old address mapped, the domain moved to your name if it isn't already, and the site registered with Google and Bing the day it launches. Then you cancel. [More on websites](/help/websites/).
