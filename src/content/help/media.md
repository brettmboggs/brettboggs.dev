Customers want to see who's coming to their house, what the work looks like and what they're buying. A phone photo in bad light tells them less than you think.

## What you get

- **A photo day.** I come to your shop, your job site or your counter. Products, work in progress, finished jobs, the team. About forty edited photos, sized for your site and your Google profile.
- **A short video.** One clip, thirty to sixty seconds. A walk through the shop, a job from start to finish, or one product shown properly. Cut once for your website and once for social.
