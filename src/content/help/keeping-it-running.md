Software you own still needs someone looking after it. Not much, but someone. This is that.

## What's in it

- Hosting and backups for your site and systems
- Fixes, usually before you notice
- Small edits whenever you ask
- Two to four Google posts a month
- Drafted replies to your reviews
- A one-page report each month: what happened, what's next
- A thirty-minute check-in
- **One practical improvement every quarter**, like a missed-call text-back or quote follow-ups

## What it isn't

A help desk. I don't fix printers or set up laptops. I look after the systems your business runs on, and keep making them fit how you work as you grow.

## Who you'd be calling

Me. Not a ticket queue, not an account manager. The person who built it and knows why it works the way it does. [Why it's one person](/help/one-person/).
