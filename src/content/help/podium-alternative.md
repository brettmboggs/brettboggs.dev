Reviews-and-messaging platforms like Podium and Birdeye sell a bundle: review requests, a shared text inbox, a web chat widget, payment links. They don't publish prices. The calculator on [my homepage](/#overhead) uses $399 a month, a third-party estimate. It's often more, on a contract.

Most local businesses use two pieces of that bundle: asking for reviews, and texting customers.

## What replaces it

- **Review requests** sent after every finished job, with the link to your Google form
- **Two-way texting** on a number in your name
- **Missed-call text-back**, so a call you couldn't take doesn't become a lost customer
- **A counter card** with a QR code that opens your review form
- **Drafted review replies**, if you're on the monthly plan

You pay the carrier for texts directly, at cost. There's no platform in the middle.

## Proof

[Mattress Concepts](/work/websites/mattress-concepts/) got a review QR card, a ready-to-send review text, and sample replies as part of their rebuild. They'd been getting one to three new reviews a year.
