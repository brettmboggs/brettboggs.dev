Jobber is built for lawn care, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing and dozens of other trades at once. It does a lot. Most crews use a third of it and pay for all of it, and the bill grows every time you add a person.

The calculator on [my homepage](/#overhead) uses $189 a month for a scheduling app like this, a typical list price in October 2026. Put in what you actually pay.

## What replaces it

- **Scheduling and dispatch** that matches how you route your day
- **Quotes** that send, follow up and turn into jobs
- **Invoices and payments**, connected to your books
- **Client history**, notes and photos in one place
- **Reminders** to clients before you show up
- **A phone app** for the crew

All of it in accounts with your name on them. No per-user fees, so hiring doesn't raise your software bill.

## Proof

[Knock Em' Down Detailing](/work/websites/ked-detailing/) runs booking, quotes, invoicing, the books and follow-ups on one system built in place of a field-service app and QuickBooks.

A free look, a written plan with a fixed price, the build while Jobber keeps running, then you cancel when you've seen it work. The [full picture](/help/custom-software/).
