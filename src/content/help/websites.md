For most local businesses, the website and the Google profile are the front door. Most of the ones I see are slow on a phone, years out of date, and paying a builder every month for the privilege.

## The site

- **Fast on a phone.** That's where your customers are.
- **Your real material.** Your photos, your actual reviews with names, the work you're proud of.
- **One clear action.** Call, book, or ask for a quote, depending on how you sell.
- **Yours.** The domain and the code are in your name. No monthly builder fee.

## The Google profile

Often more people see this than the site. It gets the right hours and holiday closures, a description that says what you do, your services and products with photos, and the first new posts. I list you on Bing Maps too, which feeds ChatGPT and Copilot.

You also get a counter card with a QR code that opens your review form, a ready-to-send text asking customers for a review, and sample replies.
