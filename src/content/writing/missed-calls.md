When a new customer calls and you can't pick up, they rarely leave a message. They hang up and call the next name on the list. You never find out who they were.

## Do the arithmetic once

Use your own numbers. As an example: a business misses six calls a week from new customers. A third of them would have booked. The average job is $400.

That's two jobs a week, $800, around $40,000 a year. Even if the real figure is a quarter of that, it's more than most owners spend on all their software.

You can find your own count. Your phone carrier or phone app keeps a missed-call log. Go through last month and mark the numbers you don't recognize.

## The fix

**Text them back, automatically, within a minute.**

> Sorry I missed you, this is Dave at Northside Plumbing. I'm on a job. What can I help with? I'll call you back within the hour.

People who wouldn't leave a voicemail will answer a text. Now you have their number and what they need, and they're less likely to keep dialing.

Some things that make it work:

- **Use your name.** It should read like you typed it.
- **Promise a time and keep it.** "Within the hour" only works if it's true.
- **Only text unknown numbers.** Your regulars and your supplier don't need it.
- **Turn it off when you're open and staffed.** It's for the calls nobody can take.

## What you need

Most business phone services can do this already, or with one add-on. If yours can't, a small connection between your phone line and a texting service does it. Business texting in the US has to be registered with the carriers (it's called 10DLC), so whoever sets it up should handle that, or your texts quietly stop arriving.

It's usually an afternoon of setup, and one of the first things worth checking in an [operations review](/help/operations-review/). It's also a typical one-process [automation](/help/automations/).

## Before you buy a platform

Some all-in-one texting and review platforms sell missed-call text-back as part of a bundle that costs hundreds a month. If it's the only feature you'd use, you don't need the bundle.
