SLUMBIO, NOTES FOR APP REVIEW

No accounts and no sign-in of any kind, so there are no demo credentials to
give and nothing to delete. Everything works from first launch. No
user-generated content, nothing shared between users, and saved mixes and the
journal live on the device only.

NEW IN 1.1
Apple Health, write only and off until switched on in Settings, Health. It
writes one asleep sample per finished night and never reads. Both purpose
strings are present because the upload validator asks for both.
Siri and Shortcuts: wind down, play a mix by name, breathe, timer, stop.
A home screen widget and two Control Center buttons, which hold no data, carry
no entitlements, and open the app through the slumbio:// URL scheme.

1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A sleep sound app for adults who have trouble falling asleep or sleep somewhere
noisy. Most sleep apps loop audio, and once you hear the loop you cannot stop
hearing it; Slumbio synthesises its sound on the device so it never repeats.
Around that sit a sleep timer, guided breathing, a sunrise alarm and a local
record of hours slept.

2. HOW TO USE IT
Nothing to set up. No login, no sample files, no permissions at launch. Three
first-run screens, then four tabs:
Tonight. The player: play, sleep timer, volume, and the wind-down routine. The
gear at the top opens Settings, the moon opens bedside mode.
Sounds. The library. 35 sounds, 12 free and playable in full. Tap a name to
start it, tap a second to layer it. Plus sounds are dimmed and play for 45
seconds before the ask.
Breathe. Guided breathing. Swipe the carousel to change pattern and the ring
above it redraws to match. 4-7-8 and Box are free.
Rest. The sunrise alarm, the bedtime schedule, a chart of recent nights, and
short sleep notes.

The sunrise alarm needs the app to be playing. iOS does not wake a suspended
app to start audio, so it ramps up only from an already running session, which
the app says on screen. To see it: start a sound on Tonight, set the alarm a
few minutes out on Rest, and leave the app in the background still playing. The
only runtime permissions are notifications, when the alarm or a bedtime
reminder is on, and Health if that switch is turned on.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None, and no network requests of any kind. All sound is generated on the device
by its own audio engine: 33 of the 35 synthesised in real time, the other 2
bundled files rendered by the developer. No data providers, third-party SDKs,
analytics, crash reporting, advertising, tracking, authentication or machine
learning. The only payment path is Apple In-App Purchase through StoreKit 2.
Three buttons open Safari.

4. REGIONS
No differences. Every feature is identical in all regions, English only, with
prices following Apple's price points per storefront.

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry. Not a medical device, no health claims, no diagnosis
or treatment. The breathing screen and the sleep notes each carry a disclaimer
saying they are not medical advice and pointing to a doctor. No third-party
protected material: all audio is generated by the app or rendered by the
developer, all artwork and copy are original, and the icons are SF Symbols.

6. IN-APP PURCHASE
One thing to buy, Slumbio Plus, sold three ways, all unlocking the same
features. Reviewed and approved with version 1.0, unchanged in 1.1.
Yearly 19.99, first week free. Monthly 3.99. Lifetime 39.99 once,
non-consumable, Family Sharing on.
Plus adds: all 35 sounds instead of 12, six layered at once instead of two, all
6 breathing patterns instead of 2, wind-down routines, the sunrise alarm and
bedtime schedule, unlimited saved mixes, and the whole journal.
To reach the purchase screen: Settings then the Plus row; or Sounds then any
dimmed name; or Breathe, swipe to Coherent and press Unlock with Plus; or Rest
then Sunrise alarm. It shows all three products with price, duration and
renewal terms, has Restore, and links to privacy and terms.
