SLUMBIO, NOTES FOR APP REVIEW

No accounts. No registration, login or sign-in of any kind, so no demo
credentials are needed and nothing to delete. Everything is available from
first launch.

No user-generated content. Nothing is shared between users. Saved mixes and the
sleep journal are stored on the device only, so there is nothing to report or
block.

1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Slumbio is a sleep sound app for adults who have trouble falling asleep or who
sleep somewhere noisy. Most sleep apps loop audio, and once you hear the loop
you cannot stop hearing it. Slumbio synthesises its sound on the device, so it
never repeats. Around it sit a sleep timer, guided breathing, a sunrise alarm
and a local record of hours slept.

2. HOW TO USE IT
Nothing to set up. No login, no sample files, no permissions at launch. Three
first-run screens, then four tabs:
Tonight. The player. Tap a sound to start it, tap a second to layer it, drag
the faders to balance. Sleep timer and bedside mode are here. The gear at the
top opens Settings.
Sounds. The library. 35 sounds, 12 free and playable in full. The rest carry a
Plus mark and preview for 45 seconds.
Breathe. Guided breathing. 4-7-8 and Box are free, four more come with Plus.
Rest. Sleep tips, the journal, and the alarm and bedtime settings.

The sunrise alarm needs the app to be playing. iOS does not wake a suspended
app to start audio, so the alarm ramps up only from an already running session,
which the app says on screen. To see it work: start a sound on Tonight, set the
alarm a few minutes out on Rest, and leave the app in the background with sound
playing. The only permission requested is notifications, when the alarm or a
bedtime reminder is on.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests of any kind. All sound is generated on
the device by the app's own audio engine. 33 of the 35 sounds are synthesised
in real time; the other 2 are audio files bundled inside the app, rendered by
the developer. No data providers, third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting,
advertising, tracking, authentication service, or machine learning services.
The only payment path is Apple In-App Purchase through StoreKit 2. Nothing
leaves the phone. Three buttons open Safari to the developer's site for
support, privacy and terms.

4. REGIONS
No differences. Every feature and every sound is identical in all regions.
English only. Prices follow Apple's price points per storefront.

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry. Not a medical device, no health claims, no diagnosis
or treatment. The breathing screen and the sleep tips each carry a disclaimer
stating they are not medical advice and pointing to a doctor. No third-party
protected material: all audio is generated by the app or rendered by the
developer, all artwork and copy are original, and the icons are SF Symbols.
