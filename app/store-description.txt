Most sleep apps play you a recording on a loop. Given long enough, you start hearing the seam. Slumbio mostly does not have one.

Thirty-five sounds, and thirty-three of them are made as they play, from noise and filters, one frame at a time. Rain that never falls the same way twice. A fire that crackles when it feels like it. Wind that gusts and settles on its own schedule. Two are pre-rendered files, and they are the only two here that repeat.

Layer up to six of them. Each has a level and two controls that reach into the sound itself, so a light rain becomes a downpour, or a room becomes a bigger room.

BREATHING YOU CAN SEE

A soft body of light sits behind every screen. In a breathing session it is the instruction: the screen fills on the inhale and empties on the exhale, with a tap at each turn and a breath sound under it.

4-7-8, box, coherent, long exhale, the physiological sigh, or numbers you set yourself. Two to fifteen minutes. A session ends at the end of a cycle, never mid-breath.

ONE BUTTON FOR THE WHOLE NIGHT

Wind down chains a breathing session into your mix and then the sleep timer. Press it on the couch and do not touch the phone again.

ALSO IN HERE

A bedside clock at two percent brightness, with the controls hidden until you touch it. A sunrise alarm that climbs out of silence instead of stabbing. A sleep journal that stays on the phone. Thirty short, practical notes on sleep, one each night.

WHAT IS NOT IN HERE

No account. No server. No analytics. No tracking. No network code of any kind. Nothing you do in Slumbio leaves your phone, because there is nowhere for it to go.

FREE, AND MEANT TO STAY USABLE

Twelve sounds, two layers, two breathing patterns, the sleep timer, bedside mode and every note are free forever. Plus adds the rest: all thirty-five sounds, six layers, every pattern, wind-down routines, the sunrise alarm and your whole sleep history.

None of this is medical advice. If sleep has been a problem for weeks, or you have a heart or breathing condition, talk to a doctor.
