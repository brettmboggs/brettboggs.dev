How to measure

Measure twice. We’ll cut once.

Width and length

Measure the platform or frame edge to edge, at the widest and longest points. Round down to the nearest half inch. A mattress that’s a hair small fits; one that’s a hair big doesn’t.

Corners

For an angled corner, measure how far the cut runs along each edge. For a rounded corner, lay a straightedge along both sides and measure from where they’d meet to where the curve starts.

V-berths

Measure the width at the head (narrow end) and at the foot, then the length down the middle. If the sides curve, a paper template traced on the berth is best. Bring it in and we’ll work from it.

Height

Check how much room you have: under a cabinet, a bunk above, or a slide-out that retracts over the bed. That decides how thick the mattress can be.
