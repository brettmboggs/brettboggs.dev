Adjustable beds

Raise your head, raise your feet.

An adjustable base replaces the box spring and lets you set the head and foot of the bed to any angle. We carry Rize and SmartFlex, from a simple head lift up to full-body massage, at a fraction of the big-store price.

Why people buy them
Snoring
Raising your head a little opens the airway.
Acid reflux
Sleeping on an incline keeps it down.
Back pain
Lifting the knees takes pressure off the lower back.
Swollen legs
Raising the feet helps circulation.
Reading & TV
Sit up in bed without a stack of pillows.
