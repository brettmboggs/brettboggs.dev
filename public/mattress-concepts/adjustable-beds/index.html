Adjustable beds

We carry adjustable beds to improve your sleep.

An adjustable base replaces the box spring and lets you set the head and foot of the bed to any angle. From a simple head lift up to full-body massage, at a fraction of the big-store price.

For the RV or camper

Uplift, a head-up base for the camper.

Uplift by Boyd Sleep lifts the head of the bed, so you can sit up in the camper without a stack of pillows. It’s light, simple and fits the bed you already have.

Head up, head down, flat
A wireless remote raises the head of the bed so you can sit up to read or watch TV.
10-minute setup
No tools. It weighs about 40 lb and comes in a single box.
Zero clearance
