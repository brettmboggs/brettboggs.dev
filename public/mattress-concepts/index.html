About us

40 years in the bedding business.

Mattress Concepts is a family-owned mattress store with on-site mattress manufacturing on High Ridge Blvd. We sell name-brand mattresses at wholesale prices, and we build custom mattresses for RVs, boats, antique beds, horse trailers and anything else that won’t take a standard size.

When you come in, you’ll deal with Clinton. Customers mention him by name in review after review: honest, knowledgeable and never pushy. We’ve built this business on referrals, and plenty of people come back for their second, third and fourth mattress.

Adjustable beds

We carry adjustable beds to improve your sleep.

Raise your head and feet to any angle. People buy them for snoring, acid reflux, back pain and swollen legs, or just to read and watch TV in bed. We carry bases with the features people want, at a fraction of the big-store price. Come try one.

Home of the two-sided mattress

2-sided flippable mattresses.

Most stores stopped selling them years ago. We didn’t. A two-sided mattress is quilted on both faces, so you can flip it as well as rotate it. It wears evenly and lasts longer.
