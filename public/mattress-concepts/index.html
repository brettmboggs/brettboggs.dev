What we build

Mattresses for the beds a store doesn’t stock.

  1. RVs and campers

    Short queens (60″ × 74″), bunks, and beds with cut or rounded corners to clear a wall or a slide-out. Firm on one half and soft on the other if the two of you can’t agree.

  2. Boats

    V-berths and cabin bunks, built to the shape of your boat instead of the nearest standard size.

  3. Antique beds

    Three-quarter beds, short lengths and the odd widths old frames were built for. Bring the measurements, or the old mattress, and we’ll match it.

  4. Horse trailers

    Mattresses for the sleeping area in the nose of a gooseneck or living-quarters trailer, cut to its shape.

  5. Odd sizes at home

    Sofa sleepers, daybeds, platform beds and anything else a store-bought size won’t fit.

Home of the two-sided mattress

A mattress you can flip.

Most mattresses sold today have one sleeping side. You can turn them end to end, but never over. A two-sided mattress is quilted and cushioned on both faces, so you flip it every few months and wear both sides evenly. It stays comfortable longer.

The big stores mostly stopped carrying them. We never did. Come lie on a few. Sets start at $179.
