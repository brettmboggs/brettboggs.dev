KED Coatings · St. Louis

Garage floors from car people.

Your car has a ceramic coating. It’s parked on a cracked, stained slab.

Scroll to see how it’s done

  1. Before

    The slab you park on.

    Oil soaked in, cracks, salt damage at the door. A coating is only as good as what it’s stuck to.

  2. Job day

    Everything out.

    The garage gets emptied first, so every inch gets the same treatment.

  3. 01 Grind

    Ground, not etched.

    Diamond grinding opens the concrete so the coating can bite. Cracks filled, spalls patched, flush with the slab.

  4. 02 Base coat

    Wall to wall.

    Rolled into the fresh grind and up the stem wall.

  5. 03 Flake

    Full broadcast.

    Thrown until no base shows through, then scraped smooth.

  6. 04 Top coat

    Clear polyaspartic.

    The layer that takes the hot tires, the salt and the spills.

  7. Done

    One day of work.

    Everything goes back on a floor that looks like this.
